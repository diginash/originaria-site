What Originária is

Originária is Aussie Coffee’s social responsibility platform—a not-for-profit social innovation initiative that identifies and strengthens grassroots social, environmental, and cultural projects in Brazil.

It operates as a practical incubator and think tank, providing free consulting and long-term support when promising ideas lack the structure, capital, and operational stability to move forward. It also functions as a support network for vulnerable individuals and groups.

Originária is designed as a shared vehicle—able to grow beyond any one individual—so that its structure, governance, and mission can endure, scale, and be used by others over time.

Why Originária exists

Originária helps promising ideas get off the ground—and then survive their earliest stage. In Brazil, young people with vision and purpose often face heavy bureaucracy, financial pressure, and complex administration simply to do things the right way.

Originária provides practical consultation and hands-on guidance—supporting testing, structure, risk management, and operational maturity—so initiatives can launch responsibly, become stable, and be ready to grow.

Staying relevant

Originária is intentionally designed to be dynamic and responsive. It can rapidly onboard new ideas, partnerships, and opportunities as they emerge, directing resources to where they can generate the greatest impact in real time. This agility supports a strong focus on effectiveness and efficiency—maximising social return and meaningful outcomes per resource invested.

Originária also serves as a springboard for the entrepreneurial ideas of its founder, Daniel. Guided by the belief that entrepreneurship can and should be used to do good, the platform enables projects that combine commercial thinking with social purpose—demonstrating that organisational viability and positive impact are mutually reinforcing.

Impact over scale

Impact, rather than scale, is the primary criterion for action.

Originária does not apply rigid thresholds for what qualifies as a programme or project. Support can range from structured initiatives to discreet, localised assistance—practical, fiscal, or professional—when a timely intervention can restore stability and continuity for an individual, family, or community.

At its core, Originária is about creating opportunity: offering young Brazilians a practical entry point into business, governance, and execution, while contributing meaningfully to the communities in which they live and work.

Impact, rather than scale, is the primary criterion for action.
