How We Create Impact

Originária works with people, projects, and partners at different stages, adapting its role to what is needed to turn intention into action.

Supported Projects (Talent Accelerator)

Originária supports externally initiated projects by offering guidance, consultation, and, where necessary, targeted financial support to help ideas get off the ground.

Support is focused on helping individuals or small teams move from intention to action—clarifying the idea, addressing practical obstacles, and providing the minimum structure or resources needed to initiate the project responsibly.

Depending on the context, this support may include advisory input, operational guidance, or limited financial aid to unlock progress. There may or may not be any expectation of financial return, depending on the nature and objectives of the project.

Flagship Projects

Originária initiates and develops its own flagship projects, and may also co‑administer supported projects that evolve into flagship initiatives over time.

In these cases, Originária plays a deeper role in shaping strategy, governance, and delivery.

For flagship initiatives, Originária may conceive the original idea or enter into shared administration once a project reaches sufficient scale or complexity.

It may retain ongoing board participation or governance involvement to ensure mission alignment, continuity, and long-term stability.

Funding and Growth Support

Originária supports projects to access funding and grow responsibly. This includes coaching and accountability around grants and incentives—such as Australian Government programs (for example, COALA), Brazilian public funding, and cultural incentive mechanisms like Lei Rouanet—helping teams stay organised, motivated, and compliant as they secure and manage resources.

Individual Stabilisation Support

Alongside project-based work, Originária supports individuals who are already demonstrating strength, capacity, and future potential, but whose lives have been disrupted by serious personal setbacks.

This support is focused on people who have invested in themselves—through education, work, and personal development—but have been pushed into financial or emotional precarity by experiences such as violence, coercion, or sustained interference from others.

Originária’s role is to help remove the immediate obstacles that prevent recovery and forward momentum. Support is practical, targeted, and discreet, aimed at restoring continuity rather than creating dependency.

Assistance may include short-term financial relief, access to essential tools for work or study, help resolving urgent debts, access to qualified therapists or psychiatrists, or trusted referrals that support safety, independence, and long-term stability. Decisions are made case by case, prioritising dignity, safety, confidentiality, and the individual’s own agency.

Cultural and Racial Literacy

Originária also develops and facilitates spaces for cultural and racial literacy, particularly for immigrants, international residents, and organisations operating in Brazil.

These activities aim to support deeper understanding of Afro-Brazilian history, resistance, ancestrality, and contemporary social realities, helping participants engage with Brazilian culture in informed and respectful ways.

This can include free or paid workshops, talks (palestras), and guided conversations on topics such as racial literacy, Afro-Brazilian spiritual traditions, cultural syncretism, and the historical and social meanings embedded in popular expressions like Carnaval.

The objective is not instruction or policing, but shared learning—building awareness of context, symbolism, and power so that cultural engagement does not unintentionally reproduce harm or erasure.

Impact, rather than scale, is the primary criterion for action.
