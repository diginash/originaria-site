Ethics, Safeguards and Governance

Originária’s social and environmental projects are intentionally aimed at vulnerable communities and people living in situations of everyday precarity. The platform upholds principles of racial literacy, care, and responsibility, recognising historical inequities and the need for culturally informed, respectful engagement—particularly when working with marginalised communities and Indigenous peoples.

Originária is committed to ethical representation and protection. Anonymity is respected wherever necessary, especially when working with children or individuals in sensitive or vulnerable situations. The organisation avoids exploitative narratives, does not expose faces or personal stories for publicity when inappropriate, and ensures that communities are not instrumentalised for visibility or fundraising. The work is guided by respect, consent, and accountability rather than visibility.

Strong governance is central to Originária’s model. The organisation will maintain an active board and fiscal council (conselho fiscal) that serve as its ethical and strategic compass—providing oversight, guidance, and a clear sense of direction. These bodies ensure transparency, integrity, and long-term alignment with Originária’s values. In certain cases, selected individuals may be compensated for fiscal or governance roles where ongoing technical, compliance, or oversight work is required.

Impact, rather than scale, is the primary criterion for action.
