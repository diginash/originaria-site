Corporate ESG Partnerships

Originária provides a ready-made structure for companies that want to deliver social and environmental impact but do not have the time, internal teams, or systems to design and manage programmes themselves.

This includes feasibility work, project design, implementation support, and long-term oversight for social, environmental, and cultural initiatives aligned with ESG objectives. Companies can use Originária as a partner to establish an immediate, credible ESG presence grounded in local context and clear governance.

Fees from corporate engagements are reinvested into Originária’s not-for-profit work. No profits are distributed for private gain.

Impact, rather than scale, is the primary criterion for action.
