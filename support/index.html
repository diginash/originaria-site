Ways to Support the Work

Alongside project delivery, Originária is sustained through a mix of internally generated revenue, direct support, and external funding. Revenue generated through Originária’s own projects and partnerships is reinvested directly into its work.

In many cases, Originária also provides direct, non‑recoverable support to projects or individuals where a timely contribution can unlock impact, without expectation of financial return. Donors and partners can additionally support impact through a range of external funding pathways.

Donations and Sponsorship

Companies and international donors can support Originária either by contributing funds that are directed to existing projects where they are most needed, or by working with Originária to support, design, and fund a specific project of shared interest.

Legacy Giving

Originária can work with donors and their advisors to shape legacy projects funded through planned giving or estates, translating personal values into lasting programmes.

Crowdfunding

Originária can coordinate crowdfunding campaigns for specific projects, providing structure, communication, and transparency so supporters understand what their contributions enable.

Impact, rather than scale, is the primary criterion for action.
